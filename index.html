The 3-question rule

  • Is the temperature changing? Use Q = mcΔT
  • Is the state changing? Use Q = mL
  • Both? Split into stages, work each out, add them up.

    Heating power: series vs parallel (same battery, same resistors)

    Battery life: same battery, two circuits (imagine a battery that stores 10 000 J)

    The circuit toolkit

    V = I × R
    P = V × I
    E = P × t

    Rearrange: I = V ÷ R. Volts × amps = watts. Watts × seconds = joules.

    Series

    • One path, so the same current everywhere
    • Voltage is shared (equal resistors: half each)
    • Resistances add: R = R₁ + R₂

    Parallel

    • Each branch gets the full voltage
    • Current splits, then adds back up
    • Total resistance is smaller, so more current and more power
    • Disadvantage: battery drains faster

    Unit check

    minutes × 60 = seconds

    hours × 3600 = seconds

    Energy is in J, power in W, current in A, voltage in V, resistance in Ω.

    Merit sentence: energy change

    "Electrical energy is transformed into thermal energy in the resistors." One line, answering exactly what was asked.

    Excellence sentence: parallel vs series

    "Ruby’s jacket is warmer. In parallel the total resistance is lower (0.925 Ω vs 3.7 Ω), so the total current is higher (8 A vs 2 A). Since P = VI, the power is 59.2 W vs 14.8 W: 4× the heating power. However, this puts a bigger demand on the battery, so it drains about 4× faster and could go flat."

    Merit sentence: efficiency (Q2c)

    Either answer is accepted if it is justified:

    • Yes: "It could be 100% efficient because no other forms of energy are produced, only thermal energy, which is the useful form."
    • No: "It is not 100% efficient because some thermal energy is lost to the surroundings and doesn’t warm Alex."

    Sharing voltage between resistors is not a reason.

    How Q2 is marked

    • (a), (b): correct answer only
    • (c): any one idea = A, justified answer = M
    • (d): A: correct voltage or power · M: correct power + attempt at energy · E: correct answer with units
    • (e): A: warmer / more current / less resistance / more power · M: more power + more current or battery flattens · E: power → current → lower resistance + battery demand

      The Q1 toolkit

      E = mgΔh
      E = ½mv²
      P = E ÷ t

      g = 10 N/kg. Rearrange: t = E ÷ P, v = √(2 × KE ÷ m). Efficiency = useful out ÷ total in.

      Energy chain (Q1a)

      Start with the source: electrical → kinetic → gravitational potential (plus some heat and sound).

      Height, not distance

      Δh is the vertical height gained. Q1(e) used 50 m as the distance walked and got 0.25 and 0.56 m/s. Both routes climb the same 50 m.

      Perfect vs real

      • Perfect: all the energy does the job.
      • Real: some is lost as heat and sound (friction, air resistance, the motor).
      • So the real lift is slower, and the real speed is lower.

      Merit sentence: Q1(c)

      “Alex is right for a perfect lift: t = E ÷ P = 149 450 ÷ 6000 = 24.9 s. But Ruby is right for the real lift: some of the motor’s energy is lost as heat and sound, so less useful power lifts the skiers, so the ride takes longer than 25 s.”

      Merit sentence: Q1(d)

      “With no losses, all 26 400 J of GPE becomes KE, so v = 28.3 m/s. In reality friction (skis on snow) and air resistance turn some of the energy into thermal and sound energy, so less becomes kinetic energy and her real speed is lower.”

      Excellence sentence: Q1(e)

      “Alex: 30 500 J ÷ 90 s = 339 W. Ruby: 33 000 J ÷ 200 s = 165 W. Ruby’s route used about half the power, so it was easier: the longer, gentler path spreads the energy over more time. The trade-off is that it took longer (200 s against 90 s).”

      Unit check

      Energy J, power W (= J/s), time s, speed m/s, height m, mass kg. Write the unit on every answer.

      Q1 answer checklist

      • Underline the command word: describe, calculate, explain, compare.
      • Calculations: formula, substitution, answer with unit.
      • Explain = claim + because + so.
      • Compare = both numbers, then a sentence naming the winner and why.
      • Cross out wrong working neatly.

      Doubling rules

      • Speed ×2 means KE ×4 (v is squared).
      • Same energy in half the time means power ×2.
      • Height ×2 means GPE ×2.
      • Losses change the useful power or the kinetic energy, never the skier’s GPE.

      Numbers from your mock

      149 450 J · 24.9 s · 26 400 J · 28.3 m/s · 339 W · 165 W · 0.5 × the power. If your answer is wildly different, re-check the unit and the height.
